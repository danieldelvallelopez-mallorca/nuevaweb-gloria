Beneath Glòria

A hidden sanctuary
beneath the house.

Beneath Glòria, stone passages lead to a heated pool extending into the old cistern of the house. Surrounded by marés stone and soft light, it is a quiet place to slow down and disconnect from the rhythm of the city.
