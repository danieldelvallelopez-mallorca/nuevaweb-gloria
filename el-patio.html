El Patio de Glòria

From morning
to evening.

El Patio changes with the light of each day. Around the original well of the house, the courtyard becomes a place of peace and serenity: breakfast in the morning, a slower pause in the afternoon, and dinner, cocktails and music in the evening.

Whether you are staying at Glòria or simply joining us in Palma, El Patio is a place to meet, linger and enjoy the house at your own pace.

The Food

Made for the table.

Seasonal ingredients and Mediterranean flavours shape the cooking at El Patio de Glòria. A menu designed to be enjoyed naturally, around the table, with the quality of the product always at its centre.

Good ingredients. Thoughtful cooking. No unnecessary complications.
