The Rooms

Each room has a
character of its own.

No two rooms at Glòria are quite the same. The architecture of the house gives each space its own character, while individually selected antique furniture, original works by Mallorcan artists and carefully chosen materials make every room feel distinctly its own.

Contemporary comfort is woven naturally into each space, without taking away from the character of the house.
